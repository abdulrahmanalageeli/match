# Personal-data and cybersecurity incident procedure

1. Contain: disable affected endpoint/account, revoke exposed credentials, preserve relevant 90-day audit records, and stop destructive cleanup.
2. Assess: timeline, systems, data categories, number/location of people, likely harm, recovery status and evidence reliability.
3. Escalate immediately to the privacy and security owners. Engage legal counsel for PDPL/NCA notification decisions and deadlines.
4. Notify the competent authority and affected individuals when the applicable Saudi requirements and risk threshold require it. Use SDAIA's official personal-data breach channel; meet any NCA reporting duty applicable to the entity.
5. Recover: patch, rotate, restore from verified backup, test access boundaries, and monitor recurrence.
6. Close: document decisions, notifications, root cause, corrective actions and executive approval; update DPIA/training.

Contacts to complete before production: incident lead ___; privacy lead ___; executive ___; counsel ___; Supabase/Vercel/Twilio/OpenAI support routes ___; SDAIA/NCA account owners ___.

Never include participant survey text, full phone numbers, access tokens or receipt images in chat or ordinary email incident updates.
