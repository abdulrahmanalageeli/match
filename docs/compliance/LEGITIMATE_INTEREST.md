# Legitimate-interest assessment: security auditing and safety bans

Purpose: protect participants, prevent evasion of a documented ban, detect misuse and investigate security events. This is necessary because account deletion/re-creation can otherwise defeat safety decisions, and consent could be withdrawn by an abuser.

Data is limited to the verified phone identifier/account link, decision metadata, IP/user-agent security events and outcomes. It is not used for advertising or general behavioral tracking. Alternatives such as device fingerprinting or continuous location collection are more intrusive and are not used.

Safeguards: restricted administrator access, reason and duration, duplicate badge rather than public disclosure, annual review, challenge/correction channel, short security-log retention and no survey content in audit metadata. A ban decision remains subject to human review.

Conclusion: the safety/security interest can outweigh the limited impact when these safeguards are followed. The privacy owner must re-run this assessment if new identifiers, automated banning, third-party sharing or materially longer retention are introduced.
