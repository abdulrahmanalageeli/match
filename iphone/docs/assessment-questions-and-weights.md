# Current Wudayn questions and weights

The standard assessment has 31 practical answers and 15 personality answers. The two parenting answers are skipped only when the member explicitly reports zero existing children, selects no future children and requires a partner with no existing children. The shortened assessment has 29 practical answers and 15 personality answers. An unanswered criterion, «أفضل» or «لا يهمني» keeps both parenting questions. The existing criterion covers children of any age. Residence uses only each member’s own plan. When both locations and plans are known, its existing 8 points reflect whether they have a feasible shared city, including explicitly permitted relocation cities. Historical pairs retain their original contract.

The current practical weights total 100 per direction. A selected priority multiplies its category’s topic weights by 1.35 in that member’s direction, with up to two priorities. Each observed soft structured criterion supplies two points of potential evidence. If more than six are known, they share the 12-point cap proportionately. Strict structured criteria are eligibility gates.

Each direction divides earned points by observed applicable weight. Unknown or inapplicable answers contribute neither points nor weight. Explicitly undecided children, first formal step or parenting answers also contribute neither. The displayed practical percentage uses the geometric mean of the two directions. Weighted coverage adjusts recommendation ordering by drawing incomplete estimates toward 50%. Beena has a neutral ranking multiplier for current assessments. Archived assessments keep their prior practical and ranking rules.

Luna uses «هل ترغبين في العمل بعد الزواج؟» for the wife-employment self question when the member is a woman.

## Personality statements

The five response choices are «لا يشبهني إطلاقًا»، «لا يشبهني كثيرًا»، «يشبهني إلى حد ما»، «يشبهني»، and «يشبهني كثيرًا». Each statement contributes one third of its domain’s score on the five-point response scale. Reverse scoring turns 1 into 5, 2 into 4, and vice versa. The five domain scores contribute zero weight to practical matching. The separate live personality percentage compares the 15 answers when both members completed personality-v2. It gives each domain 20% and each of its three statements equal weight. A difference of zero to four response steps earns 100, 75, 50, 25 or 0 points respectively. Reverse-keying both sides leaves their distance unchanged. The personality similarity percentage has no effect on the practical score. The optional partner range calculation below can adjust internal ordering.

### Personality contribution to recommendation order

Each of the 15 self-report items supplies one third of its domain score after reverse scoring. A separate optional partner card lets the member select lower (1–2), middle (2–4), or upper (4–5) ranges for any of the five traits. All start flexible. Ranges stay in the private personality answer map under the five explicit `partner_` keys. They sit outside the required assessment receipt.

For each range a member chooses, calculate the distance between the other person's domain mean and the nearest point in that range. A value inside the range has zero distance. Directional fit is one minus the mean distance divided by three, the largest possible gap. Each selected domain has equal weight within that direction. Both members must have complete current self-reports and each must specify at least one range. Otherwise personality has neutral ranking weight and the aggregate is omitted.

The geometric mean of the two directional fits gives the reciprocal score. A member sees the rounded score out of 100. Recommendations use the unrounded score to multiply the existing rank by 0.98 plus 0.04 times the score. Practical percentages and eligibility follow their existing rules. A high score means both people's self-descriptions fit the ranges they chose, which can cover as little as one domain each.

Individual ideal-partner matching has shown modest associations with romantic evaluations. This supports testing a small preference-fit contribution. The ranges, equal weights, combination rule and 2% cap are product decisions. Estimating marriage outcomes requires prospective validation with Wudayn pairs. ### Personality pilot

The instrument version and original A–E answers determine each 1–5 response, its scoring key and the domain mean. A missing or invalid item leaves its domain incomplete. Keep the three-item means descriptive and private.

With consented pilot responses, compare missingness and corrected item–total correlations for direct and reverse items. Examine whether `harsh_words` also loads on stress handling and whether `explore_views` also loads on consideration for others. Include `quiet_settings` in cognitive interviews with members who enjoy socialising in small groups. Assess wording effects, ordinal factor structure, test–retest stability and comparability across age and gender before revising items. Version any wording change.

For outcome validation, distinguish mutual continuation and progression to a formal step from voluntary reports of relationship satisfaction. Record the follow-up period and missing outcomes. Engagement alone cannot establish predictive validity.
