# Can a reciprocal questionnaire predict which connections progress to marriage?

**Prospective observational study protocol and analysis plan, draft 1.** The primary question is whether the personality preference score improves prediction of marriage to the specific person, beyond the practical questionnaire, among adult marriage-intent Wudayn members who choose to connect. All eligible profiles remain browsable. Each person sees the existing percentages and decides whom to contact.

## Research questions and outcomes

| Question | Population and clock | Endpoint |
|---|---|---|
| Primary. Does personality preference fit add information about marriage formation? | Both partners consented to longitudinal research, complete current marriage v4/personality v2 profiles, communication agreement at day zero. Include ordinary closures, early exits, and offline continuations. | Independently ask each person on days 365–394 whether they married **this person on or before the specified day-365 date**, even if they later separated. Two yes reports confirm marriage. Two no reports confirm absence by that date. Discordance, partial response and uncertainty remain unresolved. |
| Secondary. Does the questionnaire anticipate *mutual perceived marriage potential*? | Connections still open when the day-seven check becomes available. Both may answer privately during days 7–13 or until closure. | Both yes is positive. One explicit no rules out mutual endorsement. A missing reply or “too early” remains unknown unless the other side has ruled it out. Preserve the separate ratings. |
| Secondary. What happens along the way? | Use the day-two, week and exit windows with their respective denominators. | Day two measures enjoyment and desire to continue. Week-v2 measures repeat contact, personal fit, fit on practical topics actually discussed and an optional main influence. Keep these post-contact ratings separate from baseline predictors. Exit reports cover strengths, barriers and actual family contact. |
| Companion study. How do spouses experience the marriage? | Couples who report marrying; separately consent to a brief private survey approximately six months after the wedding. Record wedding month/date and elapsed time with appropriate precision. | Each spouse’s continuous satisfaction score, both distributions and within-couple disagreement. Record separation and nonresponse separately. Satisfaction among responding spouses is conditional on marriage and participation. |

The four-item Couples Satisfaction Index is a candidate for the companion study. The original has psychometric evidence, and an Arabic version was evaluated among Lebanese adults. Confirm the exact licensed wording, scoring, suitability for Saudi members and interpretation through local cognitive interviews before registration. Verify how respondents interpret “formal step,” “family contact” and “marriage” in Arabic, including whether “marriage” consistently means the marriage contract. Freeze that definition in both invitations. [Funk and Rogge](https://doi.org/10.1037/0893-3200.21.4.572) · [Arabic CSI-4 validation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8772182/)

## What the questionnaire contributes

The unit of prediction is an unordered pair at the moment both can communicate. Use the versioned snapshot taken at enrollment before that moment. Both people’s self and desired-partner answers enter the existing reciprocal calculations. The 16 practical topics cover family, finances, children, housing, timing, lifestyle and communication, with the existing applicability rule for parenting. The 15 personality statements produce five domain means after reverse scoring. An explicitly preferred partner range can differ from a member’s own trait level.

Fit four models on exactly the same development pairs. Model 0 assigns the development event rate to everyone. Model 1 uses the overall practical score, its availability, and six practical category alignment fractions. Each fraction is the share of applicable, covered topics aligned in both directions. Model 2 adds whether reciprocal personality fit was available. Model 3 adds its numerical value. That final comparison asks whether *what people answered* adds information beyond their willingness to select partner ranges. The scorer is symmetric if the partners exchange places.

Scale percentages to 0–1. A missing percentage uses 0.5 and its availability indicator. A category without covered topics uses 0.5; report these counts and a complete-coverage sensitivity analysis. The published assessment assigns no empirical marriage probabilities. The research model learns coefficients from observations across consenting pairs. Model 3 uses fixed L2-regularized logistic regression with C=1, an intercept, and standardization learned on the development data. Only pre-contact variables are predictors. Later messages, exit reasons, optional feedback and milestone dates cannot enter a baseline feature vector.

**Exploratory full-questionnaire model.** The optional `--exploratory` run fits one additional penalized regression. It includes each of the 16 topic states, using bilateral alignment as the reference, plus the self-score difference, number of expressed ranges scaled to 0–1, mean preferred-range gap and worst gap for each of five personality domains. It uses each direction’s desired range and the other member’s reverse-scored domain mean. Flexible domains contribute zero gap and zero coverage. This enables a test of detailed answers beyond the aggregate model. Treat any newly discovered weights as hypotheses for replication. In a larger independent study, compare similarity-only and directional-fit models and test actor/partner mixed effects for side-specific outcomes. A separate model updated after day seven would predict from day seven and require its own validation.

## Recruitment, missing outcomes and participant protection

Recruit consecutive eligible pairs with voluntary permission from both members for questionnaire linkage, private outcomes and the relevant follow-up. Make declining independent of profile visibility and membership. Keep collection consent, longitudinal contact consent and current withdrawal eligibility in a restricted consent ledger. The baseline SQL assumes this verification has already occurred. The service-role enrollment call alone does not establish consent.

Count eligible, invited, both consented, connected, closed early, reachable, prompted, one reply, two replies, conflicting replies, withdrawn and included. Record observation times and study versions. Members who closed the app connection may still have married offline. An unanswered anniversary survey supplies no negative label. Honor withdrawals, blocks and reports, suppress contact and exclude affected pairs from research extracts. Report their aggregate attrition where permitted. Keep the recontact directory separate from analysis IDs. A local research partner should obtain the applicable ethics determination and agree the retention, withdrawal, Arabic information sheet, and instrument-use terms before recruitment.

Members see percentages before choosing a connection. Results describe prediction in that environment. The study cannot establish a causal effect of trait matching or predict the outcome of all unseen pairings. Capture the score revision actually displayed if evaluating the effect of seeing a score. The current extract uses enrollment-time scores, which could differ from an earlier display.

## Sample planning and frozen evaluation

1. Run a separately designated feasibility cohort. Estimate eligible pair volume, two-sided response, event frequency, member reuse, and how often personality scores vary among pairs with similar practical scores. Publish no confirmatory lift from this cohort. Simulate candidate sample sizes using those nuisance estimates and the observed network of repeated members. As a proposed design target, seek at least 90% simulated power to detect a 0.01 paired Brier-score improvement with a two-sided 95% interval excluding zero. Also plan for calibration precision and repeat under worse retention and a rarer event rate. Select the larger enrollment target, fix dates and minimum worthwhile gain with collaborators before examining confirmatory associations. The 0.01 value is a planning assumption, not measured performance. [Prediction-model sample-size considerations](https://www.bmj.com/content/368/bmj.m441)
2. Lock an enrollment cutoff UTC and a later extraction date. The primary training cohort must have a complete 395-day observation period by cutoff. The new-member temporal test includes only later pairs with both partners absent from all earlier connections and a fully elapsed window at data lock. One person’s earlier nonresponse still counts as previous participation. Report excluded returning-member pairs separately. All windows and outcomes of the same couple stay together. Allow at least 395 further days after the last test enrollment for the primary endpoint.
3. Primary statistic is the paired reduction in Brier score from Model 2 to Model 3 on resolved held-out pairs. Lower Brier score means closer probability estimates to outcomes. Report prevalence, absolute Brier scores, AUC when defined, observed versus predicted rates in five fixed probability bands, and the mean prediction. Estimate uncertainty conditional on the frozen fitted models by resampling connected components of the test membership graph. The runner uses 2,000 seeded bootstrap draws and suppresses the interval with fewer than 20 components or one component above 25% of resolved test pairs. These are conservative protocol choices. A giant component or sparse events warrants descriptive reporting or a further independent cohort.
4. The runner bounds the paired improvement by assigning each unresolved test outcome the favorable and unfavorable possibilities. These are sensitivity bounds, not sampling intervals. Report response and calibration by score availability, exposure band and adequately represented prespecified subgroups with small cells suppressed. Add a sensitivity analysis among pairs with two replies and among pairs whose personality score was available. Interpret differences in response rates, safety exclusions and attrition explicitly. Document deviations and exploratory comparisons. Keep the primary claim to one endpoint and one comparison.
5. Before publishing a probability, seek replication in a separately recruited cohort, with calibration intercept/slope and uncertainty, development-sample uncertainty, and assessment of drift by catalog version. If personality adds little or uncertainty is wide, report that result. The practical and personality percentages in the directory keep their questionnaire interpretation.

## Reproduction and submission

Export one JSON object per line from [the restricted baseline query](marriage-study-cohort.sql). The enrollment snapshot supplies the six category fractions via its practical topic comparisons. A separate restricted annual file contains one current, consented response per introduction and side. Its fields are `request_id`, `side` (requester or recipient), `answered_at` (UTC with offset) and `married_by_day365` (yes, no or unsure). Omit nonresponses and withdrawn records. Export a baseline only for the consented cohort for the specific endpoint. The self-selected week cohort and the longitudinal cohort can differ.

Run from the repository root using Python 3.11 or later, NumPy and scikit-learn (tested with NumPy 2.1.1 and scikit-learn 1.7.0). Dates illustrate the interface and must be replaced by the registered dates.

```sh
python iphone/scripts/marriage-study.test.py
python iphone/scripts/marriage-study.py baseline.jsonl --endpoint week --cutoff 2030-01-01T00:00:00Z --as-of 2031-06-01T00:00:00Z
python iphone/scripts/marriage-study.py longitudinal-baseline.jsonl --endpoint marriage365 --followup annual.jsonl --cutoff 2030-01-01T00:00:00Z --as-of 2032-01-01T00:00:00Z
```

The local runner produces an aggregate research report, dependency versions and input/code fingerprints. Synthetic tests check chronology, member overlap, closure before day seven, absent reports and contradictory annual outcomes. Real annual capture, consent-ledger enforcement, ethics determination, sample-size simulation with pilot rates, and the spouse-satisfaction instrument are pre-recruitment or later-phase work. No live model has been trained.
